Coffee, buns and soup by the river

Kahvila Kaarna is a small café and bakery in the centre of Seinäjoki. We bake everything ourselves every morning, roast our coffee with a local roastery, and make one soup a day from what the farms nearby have to offer.

Come for a quick coffee on the way to work, or stay for lunch by the window. Dogs on a lead are welcome on the terrace.
